Chocolate Cake

Soft,moist and delicious homemade cake.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoe powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Instructions

  1. Mix all dry Ingredients.
  2. Add eggs, milk and butter.
  3. Mix until smooth.
  4. Pour into a cake pan.
  5. Bake at 180°c for 30-35 minutes.

Servings: 8 people
